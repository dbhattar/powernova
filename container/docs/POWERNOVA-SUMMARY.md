# PowerNOVA - Service Summary

## Full Summary

PowerNOVA is an AI-powered energy data intelligence platform that revolutionizes how energy professionals access and understand regulatory and operational information. Using advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) technology, PowerNOVA provides a conversational interface where users can ask questions in natural language and receive instant, accurate answers from thousands of indexed energy documents spanning all major ISO/RTO markets (CAISO, ERCOT, PJM, MISO, SPP, NYISO, ISO-NE), FERC filings, and state regulatory agencies. The platform combines comprehensive public data sources with the ability to upload and index proprietary documents, enabling teams to search across both public tariffs, interconnection procedures, market rules, and their own contracts and reports in one unified system. Every answer includes direct citations to source documents for verification and deeper exploration, while automated data collection ensures the platform stays current with the latest regulatory updates and market changes. Built for energy professionals who need quick, reliable access to critical information, PowerNOVA eliminates hours of manual document searching and replaces it with simple conversations that deliver verified, context-aware answers backed by authoritative sources.
